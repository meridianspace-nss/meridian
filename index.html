Protection System

Whipple shielding and active laser deflection protect the ring from space debris. Fire a piece of debris to see how each layer works.

Laser is on. Debris will be pushed off course before it arrives.

Whipple Shield

A thin outer bumper sits a short distance from the main hull. Fast debris hits the bumper and breaks into a cloud of tiny fragments. The cloud spreads out, so the inner wall receives a weak, wide impact instead of one sharp strike.

Active Laser Deflection

Sensors track incoming objects. Lasers heat one side of the object, causing a small amount of material to boil off and nudge its path away from the settlement. Whipple shields act as the backup layer.

Life Support Overview

Atmosphere

Oxygen is produced by water electrolysis. Carbon dioxide is removed by regenerable filters and the Sabatier reaction. Continuous monitoring keeps the air safe.

Water & Waste

Most water is recovered from showers, humidity, and other sources, then purified. Waste is sorted and recycled wherever practical.

Temperature

Heat is rejected to space through radiators. Humidity is controlled to prevent condensation. Zones can be adjusted independently.

Agriculture

Hydroponic and aeroponic farms grow fresh food and contribute a limited amount of oxygen. They are not the primary life-support system.
